Tu y tuong

Y tuong giong sap bai tren tay. Coi phan tu dau da sap. Lay key, dich phan tu lon hon key sang phai, roi chen key vao cho trong. Thuat toan on dinh.

Thuat toan chi tiet

Buoc 1: i chay tu 1 den n-1.

Buoc 2: key = a[i], j = i-1.

Buoc 3: while j>=0 va a[j]>key: dich sang phai.

Buoc 4: chen key vao a[j+1]. Neu da sap thi O(n).
